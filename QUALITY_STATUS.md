# Quality status — 3.0.0 (Experimental)

- 9 TTF files and 9 WOFF2 files are included.
- Each font has 414 glyphs and declares the expected weight.
- All fonts include GSUB and GPOS.
- The Regular weight is the reference design; the eight additional weights are algorithmically derived.
- Automated structural checks do not substitute for optical review, Arabic shaping tests, or full FontBakery checks.
- This package does not claim Google Fonts certification.
